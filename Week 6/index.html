Treks leaving this month

Small groups, local guides and fixed departure dates. Pick a route and see what the days ahead look like.

Annapurna Base Camp

10 daysModerate

Climb through rhododendron forest to a ring of high peaks, with tea houses along the way and a sunrise at the sanctuary.

Group size is limited to 10. Includes a licensed guide, a porter for every two trekkers and all permits.

Langtang Valley

8 daysModerate

A quieter valley close to Kathmandu, with yak pastures, Tamang villages and a clear view of Langtang Lirung from the top.

Starts with a day drive from Kathmandu. Best for trekkers who want big views without a long flight or a crowded trail.

Poon Hill Sunrise

4 daysEasy

A short climb on stone steps to a famous viewpoint, where Dhaulagiri and Annapurna turn pink before breakfast.

A good first trek. Steady pace, early starts on the summit morning and warm lodges every night.

Everest Base Camp

14 daysChallenging

The classic route through Sherpa villages and monasteries, with rest days built in so your body can adjust to the altitude.

Includes flights to Lukla, two acclimatisation days and a guide trained in altitude first aid.

Manaslu Circuit

14 daysChallenging

A remote loop around the eighth highest mountain in the world, crossing a high pass under some of the clearest night skies.

Needs a restricted-area permit and a registered guide, which we arrange for you before departure.

Mardi Himal

5 daysEasy

A ridge walk with Machhapuchhre close enough to feel enormous, and far fewer people than the main Annapurna trails.

Simple lodges, short walking days and a final viewpoint at high camp that is worth the early alarm.
